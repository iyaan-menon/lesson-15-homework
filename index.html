Find out about Forests, Rivers, Mountains and the Wildlife.

Forests

A forest is a large ecosystem dominated by a dense community of trees and other woody plants.

Rivers

A river is a natural stream of freshwater that flows downhill.

Mountains

A mountain is a large natural elevation of the Earth's surface that rises abruptly from the surrounding land.

Wildlife

Wildlife is animals and sometimes plants or other non-human organisms—that live and grow independently of human control.

Take care of Nature

  • Observe plants carefully
  • Do not disturb animals
  • Keep the environment clean
  • Recycle Plastics
